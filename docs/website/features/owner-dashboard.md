# Owner dashboard

**Included in Free**

Lab page: `/account/`. Data: `dashboard`, plus `favorites`, `privacy`, `renew`, `duplicate`.

## What works in 0.1.0

- Counts for listings, reviews, claims, favorites
- Demo member `demo-member` for the seeded lab (host members come later)
- Privacy and renew routes exist
- Contact / lead posts go to `contacts` and `email_log`

## What is still thin

WordPress Free dashboard is a left nav (tabs on small screens): Overview, My Listings, Reviews, Claims, Favorites, Profile. No Credits tab. Lab account is one page. Real member identity from EmDash auth is not wired. Email send is logged; host mail may be unavailable locally.

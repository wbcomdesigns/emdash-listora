# Reviews and claims

**Included in Free**

Reviews and claims are written on the listing page. You approve them in the admin. They are not posts.

## Reviews

On a listing, open **Reviews**, then **Write a review**. The visitor picks **Stars**, a **Title** (up to 120 characters), and a **Review** (up to 1,000 characters), then **Submit review**. The boxes stop at the limit.

With approval on, the review waits under Admin, Listora, Listing reviews. The page shows Pending, Published, and Rejected, a find box, and the waiting reviews. Open **Review** on a row and choose **Approve** to publish it, or **Reject** to keep it off.

The listing owner can reply from their account. The reply waits on the same Reviews page. **Approve the reply** publishes it under the review as **Reply from the owner**. **Reject the reply** keeps it off.

A visitor cannot review their own listing.

## Claims

**Claim** on a listing asks for **Name**, **Email**, and **Proof** (up to 1,000 characters). **Claim listing** sends it. The member then sees that the claim is waiting, instead of the form.

Claims wait under Admin, Listora, Claims. Open **Review** on a row and choose **Approve** to mark the listing as claimed and make that member the owner. **Reject** declines it.

## Messages

**Contact** on a listing is a message to the owner. The form asks for **Name**, **Email**, and **Message** (up to 1,000 characters), then **Send message**. Messages wait under Admin, Listora, Messages. Open **Handle** and choose **Mark as handled** to clear one. **Delete** opens the message, and **Delete this message** asks you to confirm.

The owner also sees messages in their account.

Listora turns an EmDash site into a directory of places, or of software. Visitors search and browse listings on a map. Members add listings, review, save and claim. You approve everything before it goes live.

## What you get

- **Directory and search.** Keyword and location search, filters by type, category and city, grid and list views, a featured row and a map.
- **Listing types you edit.** Ten local types plus Software, and your own. Each has custom fields (text, number, choice, link, date, yes or no) on forms, pages, cards and CSV, chips and sorts. Types without a place drop the map and address.
- **Software type.** Plugins, skills, agents, MCP servers and apps, with an Install box and live GitHub stars.
- **Member submissions.** A five-step form with a duplicate check, blocked words and an hourly limit.
- **Reviews and claims.** One review per member per listing, and an owner can claim their business. Both wait for approval.
- **Owner tools.** Owners edit their listing, add photos, read messages, reply to reviews and renew. Edits, photos and replies wait for you.
- **Photos and expiry.** A gallery per listing and optional expiry.
- **Import and export.** CSV in as drafts, 100 at a time, and out.
- **Moderation in admin.** Queues for everything that waits, a dashboard card and Settings.

## How it fits together

Listora is the engine: routes, storage and the admin screens. An EmDash plugin cannot add pages to a site, so the public pages ship as a kit that one command copies in. See Installation.

Guests browse and message. Adding, reviewing, saving and claiming need an account.

## Permissions it asks for

- Content (read, write, publish) and content types (read): listings are content on your site.
- Categories (read, assign): the form offers your own categories.
- Media (add): owner photos.
- Email (send): a short notice to an owner when messages wait or a listing is about to expire. No visitor text is emailed, and each notice can be turned off.
